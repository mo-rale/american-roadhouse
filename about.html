About American Roadhouse

OUR STORY

Hotel, Restaurant, and Roof Deck

American Roadhouse is a hotel and restaurant in Purok 5 Tugas, Guindulman, Bohol, offering guest accommodation alongside food and drinks. Our roof deck resto bar is open daily from 10:00 AM to 10:00 PM and also hosts private events and gatherings.

American Roadhouse brings guest accommodation, restaurant dining, and a roof deck together in Purok 5 Tugas.

WHAT WE OFFER

Everything Under One Roof

Hotel Accommodation

Comfortable, air-conditioned rooms for travelers and visitors.

Restaurant

Hearty mains, snacks, and drinks served daily.

Roof Deck, 10AM–10PM

An open-air bar and lounge with views over the rice fields.

Events & Gatherings

The roof deck is available to book for weddings, parties, and other events.

EVENTS ON THE ROOF DECK

A Space for Occasions

Our roof deck has hosted weddings, parties, and celebrations under string lights with views over the rice fields and mountains beyond.
