WELCOME TO AMERICAN ROADHOUSE

A Comfortable Place to Stay, Dine, and Unwind

Tucked into the rice fields of Tugas, American Roadhouse pairs simple, comfortable rooms with a restaurant and roof deck built for good food and easy company. It’s the kind of place travelers use as a base for exploring Bohol – and locals use for a night out.
